## Hello and welcome

This is a place to find curated software engineering and system design writing,
alongside detailed reviews of the courses in the UIUC Master of Computer Science
program. If you are early in your career or working through a degree while holding
down a job, it is written for you.

I write to make sense of what I am building and learning, especially the parts of
engineering that only become clear once you have broken them and put them back
together. The things nobody hands you directly are usually the things worth writing
down.

## What to expect

- **Course reviews** from the UIUC MCS program - what each class actually covers, how
  much time it really takes, and whether it is worth your credit hours
- **Software engineering** notes on testing, debugging, tooling, and working on teams
- **System design** fundamentals and the trade-offs behind them
- **AI tooling** in practice, including where it helps and where it gets in the way

The course reviews are the most concrete thing here. Course descriptions tell you what
a class claims to teach; they rarely tell you how many hours a week it costs, which
assignments matter, or who should skip it. I try to answer those questions honestly,
including when the answer is unflattering.

On the mechanics: this blog is a fully static site on GitHub Pages. Every post is
Markdown committed to git and built into static JSON that this React app reads
directly - no backend, no database, and nothing to go stale if I stop paying a bill.

Thanks for reading. New posts land here weekly.
